Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

Data controller: Paulius Petrusauskas, Vilnius, Lithuania

This policy explains how Paulius Petrusauskas (the “Developer”, “I”) handles information in the Lookaway app (the “App”). Lookaway is designed to be private by default: it has no user accounts, shows no ads, and does not use third-party analytics or tracking.

Who I am

I am the data controller for the limited processing described here. I am an individual developer based in Vilnius, Lithuania. You can reach me using the contact details at the end of this policy.

No account required

The App does not require you to create an account or to provide your name, email address, or other directly identifying information to function.

No ads, no tracking, no analytics

Lookaway does not display advertising and does not include third-party advertising, analytics, attribution, or tracking software. It does not access your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not track you across other apps or websites, so the App does not present an App Tracking Transparency prompt.

Information stored on your device

Your preferences, settings, and the answers you enter during onboarding are stored locally on your device. This on-device information is not transmitted to or collected by me, and it is removed when you delete the App.

Subscriptions

The optional Lookaway Premium subscription and any in-app purchases are processed by Apple. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; I do not receive or store your payment details. I receive from Apple only the subscription status needed to unlock paid features on your device. Apple processes this information as an independent controller under Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Legal bases

Where the GDPR applies, I rely on: performance of a contract to provide the subscription and unlock paid features (Art. 6(1)(b)); and my legitimate interests in the basic operation and security of the App (Art. 6(1)(f)). Because the App shows no ads and uses no analytics or tracking, no consent-based advertising processing takes place.

Data retention

On-device data remains until you delete it or uninstall the App. Subscription-status information is retained only for as long as needed to provide paid features; Apple retains transaction records under its own policies and applicable accounting law.

International data transfers

I do not transfer your personal data outside your region. The only third party involved is Apple, which operates globally and processes purchase data under its own policy and safeguards (such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses, where applicable).

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your personal data, and to its rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, and portability, as well as the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests and to withdraw any consent you have given at any time. Because almost all data stays on your device, deleting the App removes it. For any subscription-status data, contact me using the details below and I will respond within one month.

Complaints

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. In Lithuania this is the State Data Protection Inspectorate (Valstybinė duomenų apsaugos inspekcija, VDAI, vdai.lrv.lt). You may also complain to the authority in your EU country of residence.

Automated decision-making

I do not carry out automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects. The onboarding answers are used only on your device to suggest a routine.

Children’s privacy

The App is a general-audience product and is not directed to children, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children. In the EEA the minimum age to consent to any processing that requires consent is 16, or the lower age set by your country’s law (in Lithuania, 14); in the United States, the App is not directed to children under 13. As the App collects no personal information for its own use, no parental-consent mechanism is required. If you believe a child has provided personal information contrary to this policy, contact me and I will address it.

Changes

I may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always available at this page, with the “Last updated” date revised accordingly.
